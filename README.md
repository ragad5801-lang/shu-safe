# shu-safe
Voice-first, offline mobile health app empowering youth in rural Liberia with private access to accurate sexual and reproductive health information — built with React Native, Vosk, Mozilla TTS, and Firebas
Shu-Safe

Ask. Learn. Stay Safe — without anyone knowing.

Shu-Safe is a voice-first, offline health education app designed for youth aged 10–24 in rural Liberia.
It provides private, stigma-free access to accurate sexual and reproductive health (SRHR) information, even without internet access or literacy skills.

Problem

In many parts of rural Liberia, young people lack access to reliable sexual and reproductive health information.
This is caused by a combination of poor internet access, low literacy rates, cultural stigma, and widespread misinformation.
As a result, many youth face high risks of teenage pregnancy, sexually transmitted infections, and unsafe practices.

Solution

Shu-Safe offers a private, offline, and voice-powered way for youth to ask health-related questions in their own languages and receive trusted, spoken answers.
The app removes barriers of literacy and connectivity, ensuring that every user can access health knowledge safely and confidentially.

Key Features

Voice-based Q&A: Ask questions naturally and receive spoken answers in Liberian English, Kpelle, or Bassa.

Privacy-first: No login or personal data required. All processing happens on the device.

Offline functionality: Works entirely without internet. Syncs updates when a connection is available.

Emergency mode: Detects urgent situations (“I need help”) and provides local hotline or SMS support.

Conversation memory: Remembers the last few questions for more natural, contextual interactions.

Gamified learning: Offers short “Myth or Fact” quizzes to reinforce learning.

Local analytics: Tracks common topics and usage statistics without sending any personal data online.
